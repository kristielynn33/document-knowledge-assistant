\# Northstar Demo Company: Remote Work Policy



This is an invented policy created for a software portfolio. It does not describe a real employer.



\## Remote-work allowance



Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. This policy does not authorize fully remote roles.



\## Security requirements



Remote employees must use an approved company laptop, enable multi-factor authentication, and connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.



\## Equipment requests



Employees must request remote-work equipment through the IT service desk. Purchases require approval before an order is placed.
