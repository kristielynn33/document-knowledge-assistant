\# Northstar Demo Company: Leave Policy



This is an invented policy created for a software portfolio. It does not describe a real employer.



\## Annual leave allowance



Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a prorated allowance.



\## Carryover



Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used by March 31.



\## Leave requests



Annual leave requests must be submitted through WorkPortal at least 10 business days in advance. A manager must approve each request before leave is taken.
